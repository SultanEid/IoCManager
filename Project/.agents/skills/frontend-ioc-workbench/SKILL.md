description: Use for route mapping, page merges, navigation refactors, case-page design, table-heavy workflows, and replacing mock operational UI with real contracts. Do not use for backend-only work.
---

1. Preserve the current visual identity.
2. Optimize for analyst speed, dense readable data, and restrained motion.
3. Reduce top-level navigation sprawl.
4. Keep the case page as the center of the workflow.
5. Do not add dashboards that are generic or decorative.
6. In normal mode, never display fake deployment state, fleet health, audit history, or case state.
7. Prefer loading, empty, and dependency-down states over placeholders that look real.
